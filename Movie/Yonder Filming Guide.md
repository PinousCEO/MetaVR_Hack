# Three minutes with Nilo

This shot list follows the [English walkthrough](Yonder%20-%20Three%20Minute%20Walkthrough.mp4). The walkthrough uses the MR simulator and desktop games. For a take in your own room, record the Quest view and the TV separately. A side shot of both people makes the white player's pointing gesture legible.

Before recording, start Yonder on the computer driving the TV, connect the headset, finish screen calibration, and check that the microphone hears “Hey, Nilo.” Turn on microphone audio in the headset recording. For the first-harvest sequence, use a garden without installed upgrades. An existing garden keeps its progress.

| Time | On camera |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:11 | Launch Yonder and hold your head steady through the logo reveal. Frame Nilo on the desk. Say “Hey, Nilo” and leave room for the response. |
| 0:11–0:44 | Say “Hey, Nilo, open the star garden.” Pinch each of the three pots to plant, release, then pinch again to water. Get close enough to see the shoots. The first crop takes 45 seconds after watering. |
| 0:44–0:59 | Film one goalkeeper save and one tennis return. Keep the TV edge and the wearer's hands in the same frame so the crossing is visible. |
| 0:59–1:13 | In Gomoku, place a black stone from the headset. Film the other person pointing at an intersection on the TV. The wearer looks toward the hand and board while the aiming cursor settles and the white stone lands. |
| 1:13–1:56 | Capture Demon Gate directly on the computer. Follow an approaching enemy, fire, then hold on one augment choice long enough for the viewer to read it. |
| 1:56–2:29 | Return to the ripe garden. Harvest all three stars and frame the balance rising to nine. Buy Rain Bell for three and Sun Dial for six. Frame the installed labels. |
| 2:29–3:00 | Plant the next crop. Rain Bell waters it automatically and Sun Dial brings the wait down to 25 seconds. Cut across the wait or record the next take after reopening the garden. Harvest the three plants and buy Twin Bloom for nine. Each subsequent plant yields six stars. |

Shoot the TV games between watering and harvesting. The garden keeps time while closed. A cut between separate takes can show both the real wait and the resulting harvest within three minutes.
